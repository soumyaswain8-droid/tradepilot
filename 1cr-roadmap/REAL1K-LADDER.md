# The ₹1,000 ladder — from rehearsal to the ₹3–4k ambition, honestly

**The target restated.** ₹3–4k from ₹1k is +300–400%. In cash intraday that is ~140
perfect days; at 4× MIS it is ~30 flawless compounding days; in options it can happen
in a week — and the modal outcome there is −100% with zero measured directional edge
on our side. So the ladder below earns the right to each next rung instead of
pretending the top rung is reachable in one step.

09:25 card → Telegram → Soumya walks Kite to the ORDER CONFIRM screen and backs out →
logs `--filled` at the screen price → manages SL/target → 14:45 square-off →
`--exited`. Measures: card-to-screen latency, quote drift in that window, every
friction of the real loop. Ledger marked paper.

## Rung 1 — Tue 08-25 onward: REAL, ₹1k at 4× MIS
One card/day, exposure ~₹4,000, SL 0.8% (₹32 ≈ 3.2% of capital), target 1.5%,
hard square-off 14:45. Good day ≈ +₹40–60 real. **Gate to Rung 2: 10 sessions with
(a) fills within 0.15% of card, (b) no missed square-off, (c) cumulative P&L > −₹150.**
This gate is about OPERATIONS, not edge — edge was never proven intraday.
