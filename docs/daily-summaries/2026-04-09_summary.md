## 4. Mistakes and Root Cause Analysis

### Root Cause 1: No Circuit Breaker
**What happened:** After 5, 10, 15 consecutive losses, the system kept trading at full speed.
**Impact:** 33 losing trades executed when the system should have paused after 5.
**Severity:** CRITICAL

### Root Cause 2: No Regime Detection
**What happened:** v4 had no concept of bull/bear/sideways markets. It treated a -0.93% Nifty day the same as a +1% rally day.
**Impact:** Full-size long-only trades in a clearly bearish session.
**Severity:** CRITICAL

### Root Cause 3: Unlimited Re-Entry
**What happened:** SHRIRAMFIN was entered 4 times, each time losing money. No limit on how many times the same stock could be traded in a day.
**Impact:** Rs 11,217 lost on a single stock (36% of total loss).
**Severity:** HIGH

### Root Cause 4: Long-Only Bias
**What happened:** v4 could only buy (go long). In a falling market, every entry was fighting the trend.
**Impact:** 89% of trades lost because the market was moving against long positions.
**Severity:** HIGH

### Root Cause 5: No VIX-Based Position Sizing
**What happened:** VIX at 20.4 signals elevated uncertainty. Position sizes should have been cut by 50% or more.
**Impact:** Full-size positions amplified losses.
**Severity:** MEDIUM

## 9. Learnings

1. **Data leakage is the #1 ML risk.** An IC of 0.97 is not alpha -- it is a bug. Real intraday IC is 0.02-0.05.

2. **Circuit breakers are not optional.** Without a hard stop after consecutive losses, a bad day becomes catastrophic.

3. **Regime detection is the highest-leverage feature.** Knowing "don't trade aggressively today" is worth more than any signal improvement.

4. **VIX above 18 = reduce exposure immediately.** The market is telling you risk is elevated. Listen.

5. **Re-entry limits prevent compounding errors.** SHRIRAMFIN entered 4 times = Rs 11,217 lost. Max 1 re-entry would have capped it at Rs 3,000.

6. **Long-only in a bear market is a guaranteed loss.** v5's short selling capability is essential for bearish sessions.

7. **Trade count is a risk metric.** 37 trades in a day is too many for a Rs 10L account. Quality over quantity.

8. **Walk-forward validation is non-negotiable.** Any ML model for trading must be validated on strictly future data, never in-sample.

9. **India VIX is the single best feature.** It ranked #1 in feature importance across all model configurations.

10. **Build risk controls before alpha generation.** The order should be: risk management first, signal generation second. Today proved that a mediocre signal with great risk management (v5) beats a decent signal with no risk management (v4).

11. **GIFT Nifty pre-market data predicts gaps with 75% accuracy.** This is free alpha that v4 completely ignored.

12. **The 80% profit ratio target requires regime-aware trading.** Brute-force signal generation won't get there. Knowing *when* to trade is as important as knowing *what* to trade.
