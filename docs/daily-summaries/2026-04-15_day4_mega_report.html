L-001 | 97% Win Rate Is Achievable on Sideways Days
Day 3 (bear): 86%. Day 4 (sideways): 97%. The engine performs BETTER on mixed/sideways days than on bear days. The theory: on sideways days, stocks with strong momentum (SUZLON, JSWENERGY, BLUESTARCO) continue their move while the index stays flat — creating a perfect hunting ground for the multi-pool swing engine. Tags: win-rate, sideways, engine-calibration.

9/9 win rate, Rs +11,453, average gain +7% per trade. SUZLON has now appeared in the top performers list across multiple days. L-003 | DRREDDY Requires Immediate Blacklisting
Three consecutive stoploss hits on DRREDDY today (-Rs 412). Pattern: engine enters on positive signals, stock reverses within 10 minutes. Root cause: DRREDDY has high intraday volatility and doesn't exhibit the clean momentum profile the engine expects. Action: add to exclusion list. Review after 5 trading days of monitoring. Tags: drreddy, blacklist, stoploss-pattern.
L-004 | Undersizing in SIDEWAYS Regime Costs Rs 33,000/Day
0.6x sizing vs 1.0x = ~Rs 33,000 left on table today. The SIDEWAYS regime should use 0.8x sizing, not 0.6x — the market was generating clear momentum moves. Update regime-sizing logic: BEAR=0.3x, SIDEWAYS=0.8x, MIXED=0.9x, BULL=1.0x. The current SIDEWAYS=0.6x is too conservative. Tags: position-sizing, regime-detection, optimization.
L-005 | v5.4 Bidirectional Validates Parallel Engine Strategy
Running v5 and v5.4 in parallel on the same capital (different capital pools) generated Rs +76,567 combined vs Rs +49,713 for v5 alone — a 54% improvement in total returns. The two engines don't compete; they complement. v5 runs from 9:15, v5.4 injects fresh momentum trades from 12:00 when the afternoon session begins. This is the blueprint for the v6 multi-engine orchestrator. Tags: v5.4, parallel-engines, multi-engine.
L-006 | Exit Time Anomalies Detected — Data Quality Issue
Several trades show exit times earlier than entry times (e.g., OIL entered 14:53 but exited 10:50). These are carry-over positions from the previous session's data that were closed at the start of the current session. The data model needs a "carry_over" flag to distinguish same-day entries vs previous-day carry-forwards. This prevents misclassifying Day 3 positions as Day 4 intraday trades. Tags: data-quality, carry-over, architecture.
