# 3. The reasons — three findings

**Reason 1 — shorts bled on *both* green days (the constant).** Every engine's short book was red on both days. Shorting into a rising tape loses, period. Suppress shorts on green → both days improve.

**Reason 2 — longs are the swing, and they're execution-dependent.** 07-01 longs churned (red); 07-02 longs caught the green (classic +1,304, flip +1,587), lifting every engine +₹2,400–2,600. The tell: **v5_classic's clean-execution longs made +1,304 on 07-02, while v5_long's longs (same direction, v5's churny execution) made only +24.** Clean early-entry catches the green; churn doesn't.

**Reason 3 — v5_cut's reversal shows n=1 fragility.** It went best (+1,003) → worst (−1,977) on two identical green days, entirely via its shorts (+706 → −2,797). No short-gate is safe on a green day; *not shorting a confirmed up-tape* is more robust than shorting carefully.

## 4.4 Patterns from the trade-level data

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
The entry/exit times + the newly-logged scores reveal three concrete patterns that the aggregates hide:

- **The killer shorts all fired at the 09:06 open and stopped out within 10–20 min.** DABUR, HINDUNILVR, TORNTPHARM (07-01) and TATASTEEL, BLUESTARCO (07-02) — shorted at the open on the green tape, stopped by 09:26. *Shorts opened at the bell on an up-day are the worst trades in the book.*
- **The winning shorts held for hours.** SAIL (330m), GLENMARK (338m), BANKBARODA (195m→TARGET) — the shorts that worked were held long, not stopped fast. Fast-stop = loss, long-hold = win, on the short side.
- **The conviction score IS informative (first look).** The big-loss shorts scored **28–34**; the winning longs scored **80+** (PRESTIGE 80.2, ETERNAL 80.5, GODREJPROP 80.7). Score cleanly separates direction (high→long, low→short), and the low-score-short + open-entry combo is the systematic loser. *This is exactly the data we couldn't correlate before the 06-30 logging fix — now we can.*
- **Churn is visible per-stock:** DABUR on 07-01 was shorted (score 28.9, −756) *and* longed (score 75.8, −217) — the engine flipped its own view within 30 min and lost on both. COROMANDEL the same (LONG −320, SHORT −300).

# 7. Conclusions & the two fixes this points to

1. **Suppress shorts on a confirmed green tape (the BULL-side tilt).** Shorts bled both green days; the worst were opened at the bell. `v5_flip` handles the BEAR side only — it needs the mirror. *This is the highest-conviction fix across three sessions now.*
2. **Rebuild toward v5_classic's clean-execution longs.** The two-day gap proves clean early-entry longs catch the green while churny execution doesn't. Also fold `v5_cut`'s tighter short-gate into live v5 as the cheap, partly-validated interim.
3. **Use the now-logged conviction score.** The 06-30 fix means we can finally test whether score→P&L holds. First look: low-score shorts opened at the bell are the systematic loser — a candidate rule (don't open low-score shorts in the first 15 min of a green tape).
