Why this works for all 7 variants without editing 7 scripts

Every variant calls RiskManager.check_can_trade() before opening a position. Tonight's patch added an auto-poll inside that method: it reads the pool aggregate P&L, trips the kill-switch when it crosses Rs −5,000, and refuses entries. Scripts don't need to know — they just keep calling the method they already call.
