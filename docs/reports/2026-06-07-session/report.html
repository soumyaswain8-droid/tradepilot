§1Root-Cause Diagnosis

1.1   v4 — the May-4 overfit retrain

v4 was gross-profitable through early May, then went flat-to-negative. A controlled comparison holding the market regime constant ruled out the market:

RegimeOLD engine (pre-May-4)NEW engine (post-May-6)
SIDEWAYS+₹6,520 / day−₹5,846 / day
BEAR+₹12,993 / day+₹1,392 / day (9× worse)

The culprit (commit 1d174bc, May 4): the ML model was retrained from a 5-tree LightGBM into a 1,735-tree model on the same 22 features — a 350× complexity explosion. Real-trade quality collapsed in step: win rate 61%→38%, per-trade edge +₹141→−₹8, hold time 97→172 min. Classic overfitting.

Bias caught
An early "clean controlled experiment" leaned on 3–4-day samples (the project's own don't-rush-findings rule). The robust evidence is structural — the 350× tree explosion — not the small P&L windows. May-6's +₹196k was largely a NaN/cache price artifact, not a real day.

1.2   v5 — a fair-weather long engine

v5's code never changed (Apr-16 → May-3 identical), so its decline was market + positioning, not a regression. Its edge is long-heavy harvesting of market up-days; its short arm is regime-conditional:

v5 short armTradesAvg / tradeWin rate
SIDEWAYS217+₹16659%
BEAR416+₹339%

The engine deploys shorts most in bear (416 vs 217) — exactly where they don't work. Even in bear, v5's longs (+₹103/trade) beat its shorts (+₹3). Fix path: gate shorts out of bear, or run long-only.

1.3   The disk-full incident

Mid-session the machine was at 100% / 686 MB free — the live engine's writes were failing (empty cache dirs). Freed 2.2 GB by removing regenerable Rust build output (engine/target), likely a contributing factor to stale-cache symptoms.

§4Dashboard Rebuild

  • Unified nav — a top-right switcher (Desk · Market · Live · A/B · Agents) on every dashboard; market-state logic (OPEN only weekday 09:15–15:30 IST) synced across /live and /lab.
