PRE-REGISTRATION — overnight drift validation at transactable prices
Written 2026-09-01, AFTER drawing the sample and AFTER seeing the PAPER (close->open) drift
on that sample (mean +0.632%, median +0.478%), but BEFORE fetching a single intraday bar
and therefore before seeing any realistic-fill or netted number.

WHAT IS BEING TESTED
Buy the mover at a transactable price near the close of its +5% day; sell at a transactable
price the next morning. Net of delivery costs. Is the +0.64%/night paper drift still there?

ENTRY   = volume-weighted average of the 15:15 and 15:20 five-minute bars (15:15-15:25 IST),
          typical price (H+L+C)/3 weighted by bar volume. Chosen because it is an order you
          can actually work: 10 minutes of participation, ending 5 minutes before the close,
          entirely outside the 30-minute window the official close is averaged over.
EXIT    = close of the 09:15 five-minute bar, i.e. the traded price at 09:20 — five minutes
          after the open, deliberately NOT the pre-open auction print.
          Secondary exit reported: VWAP of the 09:15+09:20 bars (09:15-09:25).
COSTS   = 0.240% round trip (delivery) + Rs 18.80 flat DP charge on the sell.
          Headline position size Rs 8,000 (gap-risk.md §9 recommendation) => 0.235% flat,
          0.475% all-in. Also reported at Rs 24,000 (0.318% all-in) and Rs 3,000 (0.867%).

THE BAR — all four must hold at the Rs 8,000 headline size to justify building the lane:

  B1  per-trade NET MEAN            >= +0.15%/night
  B2  date-clustered HAC t-stat     >= 3.0
  B3  per-trade NET MEDIAN          >  0.00%   (a broad drift, not a tail)
  B4  top 5% of events contribute   <= 50% of total net P&L

  4/4 = BUILD.  3/4 = redesign and retest, do not build.  <=2/4 = DEAD.

B3 is the load-bearing one and it is deliberately hard: gap-risk.md §5 showed the mover
close-to-close MEDIAN is already -0.065%/day. If the median net is negative, the lane is a
right-tail lottery on a Rs24,000 book and the book will not survive the losing string long
enough to reach the tail.

FAILURE MODE THAT IS NOT A VERDICT
If Kite coverage on the sampled events is below 60%, or if coverage is systematically biased
(e.g. only the liquid survivors resolve), the answer is "undetermined", not "fail". That
threshold is fixed here, now, before any fetch.
