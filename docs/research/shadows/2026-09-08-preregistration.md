# Shadow experiments — pre-registered 2026-09-08, 09:12 IST (before the open)

Soumya's brief (2026-09-08): make the regime call itself the experiment; start the arm-band
shadow ledgers; keep DAYGAIN as a computed baseline row; do NOT touch v5's stop width.

## 1. Regime shadow (10 sessions: 2026-09-08 → 2026-09-19)

**Question.** On 2026-09-07 v5 read BEAR and v5_wide read SIDEWAYS on the same tape, and the
outcome gap was 8×. Which reading is right, measured, not argued.

**Method.** A read-only sidecar (`scripts/shadow-signal-snapshots.py`) captures v5's signal set,
regime and open positions every scan. At EOD (`scripts/shadow-regime-eod.py`) every snapshot's
signals are re-gated under the ALTERNATE regime (BEAR↔SIDEWAYS; BULL↔SIDEWAYS if it occurs)
using v5's own deployment rules — score floor, per-direction slot partition, allocation multiplier.
The two allowed-trade sets are priced from Kite 5-min candles with v5's live exit rules
(hard stop, target, trailing arm 1.0/0.5, 15:15 close). Trades common to both sets cancel;
only the symmetric difference is scored.

**Pre-registered gate.** After 10 sessions, if the alternate regime's allowed-trade set beats the
live one by **more than ₹2,000 cumulative net**, the regime classifier gets rebuilt. If not, the
live classifier stays. No other parameter is read from this experiment.

**Caveats fixed in advance.** Signals are generated by v5 under its live regime; the counterfactual
re-gates those signals, it does not re-score them under the alternate regime (scorer inputs are
regime-aware at line 1287 of v5-paper-trade.py). If the gate passes this is a lower bound on the
alternate regime's advantage; if it fails, the alternate regime also lost on gating alone.

## 2. Arm-band shadow ledgers (10 sessions, same window)

**Question.** Half the "left on the table" ceiling sits in winners that round-trip through stops
(7 of 7 measured days; 18 of 36 stops reversed on 2026-09-07 worth ₹8,055). Which trailing arm
captures it without giving back the exits that save money.

**Method.** Pure EOD replay (`scripts/shadow-armband.py`): every live v5/v5_wide entry is re-run
against Kite 5-min candles under each band, same entry price and time, same hard stop and target:

| Band | Arm (profit %) | Step (%) |
|---|---|---|
| live | 1.0 | 0.5 |
| arm0.5 | 0.5 | 0.25 |
| arm0.3 | 0.3 | 0.25 (the backtest's t=3.85 winner) |
| fixed | never arms | hard stop only |

**Pre-registered gate.** After 10 sessions, a band that beats the live band by **more than ₹2,000
cumulative net** across both engines, without a worse max daily loss, replaces the live setting.
Otherwise 1.0/0.5 stays. The live stop width is not changed during the window.
