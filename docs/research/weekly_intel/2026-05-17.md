## Top 3 Actionable Items

1. **SEBI Retail Algo Framework now fully mandatory (April 1, 2026)** — Every algo-generated order must carry a Unique Exchange Strategy ID; brokers are Principals, third-party algos are Agents. Even paper-trading platforms positioning for live-trading must architect for Strategy ID tagging + static IP + broker-routing now to avoid retrofitting. — https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/feb-2025/safer-participation-of-retail-investors-in-algorithmic-trading_91614.html — forward to: **architect**

2. **arXiv 2505.08180 — Forecasting Intraday Volume in Equity Markets with ML** — Shows intraday volume is highly predictable with ML using high-frequency predictors + commonality, with measurable economic gains via VWAP strategies. Directly relevant to Sprint 3 (features) — add volume-prediction features to Alpha158 set, plus VWAP-anchored labels as a meta-label channel. — https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08180 — forward to: **alpha-hunter**

3. **Kronos — new pre-trained market foundation model (Feb 2026 review by Kinlay)** — A finance-native pre-trained model emerging alongside TimesFM 2.5 / Chronos-2 / MOIRAI-2. Worth a 30-min eval to see if it offers a stronger zero-shot baseline than our LightGBM regressor at IC ~0.005 before we sink Sprint 3-4 into hand-crafted features. — https://jonathankinlay.com/2026/02/time-series-foundation-models-for-financial-markets-kronos-and-the-rise-of-pre-trained-market-models/ — forward to: **alpha-hunter**
