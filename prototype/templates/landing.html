Market Data Ingestion
Real-time + historical data from Indian exchanges
Live prices — NSE / BSE tick-by-tick
Volume + delivery data — exchange APIs
FII / DII daily flows — NSDL reports
Options chain — OI, volume, PCR
India VIX + global indices — fear gauge
Pulls 2-year daily history + 60-day intraday (5-min candles) for every stock in the universe
Multiple data providers with automatic failover — if one source is down, the next picks up seamlessly
Data refreshes before 9:15 AM every trading day so scores are ready at market open
⚙
Feature Engineering
19 quantitative features extracted per stock
Feature importance weights
Price action: Momentum, VWAP deviation, opening range breakout, trend strength
Volume: Relative volume ratio, volume surge detection, delivery percentage
Institutional: FII/DII flows, put-call ratio, open interest buildup, max pain distance
Relative strength: 5-day and 20-day performance vs NIFTY benchmark
Context: Sector breadth, VIX regime, market advance/decline ratio
🧠
ML Ensemble Scoring
Multiple models vote to reduce bias
Model A — Gradient boosted trees (classification)
Model B — Gradient boosted trees (ranking)
Composite — Weighted ensemble vote
Relative rank — Percentile scoring across universe
Models are retrained daily on the latest data before market open — no stale predictions
Ensemble approach reduces overfitting — no single model dominates the decision
Relative ranking ensures you always get the top picks regardless of overall market direction
Models learn from 2 years of Indian market patterns — sector rotation, FII cycles, earnings seasons
🧭
Market Regime Detection
Adapt strategy to current market conditions
BULL — Trending up. Full allocation, momentum-first.
SIDEWAYS — Range-bound. Reduce size, mean-reversion.
BEAR — Trending down. Defensive sectors, tight stops.
Analyses NIFTY trend, VIX level, advance/decline ratio, and FII flow direction to classify the regime
Each regime triggers different capital allocation — more aggressive in bull, more defensive in bear
Sector rotation is regime-aware — Energy and Pharma in bear markets, IT and Banking in bull markets
The v5 engine made +5.48% in 3 days during a BEAR regime by detecting sector rotation opportunities
👤
Human-in-the-Loop
Expert oversight at every critical step
Weight calibration — Feature weights set by domain experts
Signal review — Unusual signals flagged for manual check
Risk override — Circuit breakers tuned from trading experience
Daily validation — Paper trade results reviewed every evening
ML models propose, humans dispose — no trade goes out without passing human-defined risk rules
Feature weights are calibrated weekly based on what actually worked in the Indian market
Corporate action filters (earnings, splits, bonuses) prevent models from trading on distorted data
This is NOT a black box — every decision is transparent and reviewable
